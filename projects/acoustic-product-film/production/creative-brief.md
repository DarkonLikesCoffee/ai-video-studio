# Creative Brief — AURA

Create a 20-second cinematic launch film for a fictional premium wireless headphone. Make it tactile, restrained and memorable, without inventing product specs.

## Objective
Build desire by revealing the product's tactile design and presence rather than listing features.

## Audience and response
Design-conscious consumers who respond to premium product craft. Remember the headphone silhouette and associate it with thoughtful craftsmanship.

## Creative thesis
Reveal the headphone through material, sound and controlled movement; each shot uncovers one layer of identity until a confident hero frame.

## Art direction
- **Palette:** near-black graphite, cool brushed aluminium, muted mineral grey, subtle sea-glass teal.
- **Typography:** Restrained editorial sans-serif; short uppercase copy only when needed.
- **Composition:** Asymmetric macro crops evolving into a balanced three-quarter hero composition with deliberate negative space.
- **Lighting:** Soft directional key with narrow edge highlights and rich but readable shadows.
- **Materials:** Brushed metal, satin polymer, soft-touch ear cushions and fine acoustic perforations.

## Storytelling and motion
Open on a concealed material detail; progressively reveal the silhouette, one tactile interface detail, an expressive exploded view and then the complete headphone. Use visible visual progression rather than feature cards.

Slow macro push, a restrained orbit, a lateral detail move and a final camera move that settles into a still hero. Cuts follow shape and highlight continuity.

## Sound
Minimal original electronic pulse with tactile Foley, one quiet control click and a restrained final impact. No voice-over.

## Constraints and assumptions
- No fabricated specifications, endorsements or testimonials.
- Use original assets and record their provenance.
- Keep copy short and inside safe areas.
- No narration or voice-over.
- AURA and the headphone are fictional and created only for this visual benchmark.
- The stylized 3D model is illustrative, not a depiction of a real commercial product.
- The 20-second duration and 16:9 delivery are fixed.

## Acceptance criteria
- The headphone silhouette is fully revealed by the end of the second shot.
- At least four distinct visual scales or compositions are used.
- Every shot has a visible action or visual progression.
- The product design remains coherent across all shots.
- The final hero shot holds for at least two seconds and resolves the opening material motif.
- Original music and Foley support the edit without narration or unsupported product claims.
