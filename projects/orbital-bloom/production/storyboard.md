## Continuity and design notes
- **Seed identity:** Ovoid shell of eight copper segments, titanium underlayers, visible pins/hinges, clear chamber, amber core, machined iris and three rotating rings. Maintain silhouette through all five beats.
- **Mechanical timing:** Core ignition precedes movement. Locks release, iris blades separate, then shell plates fan outward. Avoid purposeless spinning.
- **Plant identity:** Roots emerge from the seed base; branching stems carry veined leaves; twelve translucent petals open individually around an amber heart.
- **Environment:** The cracked soil patch is inside one physical greenhouse. The final shot reveals the same structure; it is not a location change.
- **Lighting:** Cold station fill → warm core practical → botanical bounce → planetary blue in the final reveal.
- **Typography:** No labels, HUDs, chapter markers or subtitles. The final title is the only copy.
- **Review priority:** Inspect the actual MP4 for panel continuity, iris readability, growth silhouette, dome visibility and title timing. A contact sheet alone cannot judge movement.
