# Sports Car 3D Cinematic

Composition ID: `SportsCarCinematic`

- 1920×1080, 30 fps, 720 frames (24 seconds).
- Procedural Three.js vehicle geometry: sculpted low body, cabin and windows, front/rear fascia, LED lights, aerofoil, carbon-look trim, four rotating wheels with multi-spoke alloy rims.
- A red metallic finish is lit with moving red/blue studio lights against a dark reflective stage.
- The camera follows a deterministic multi-angle path around the vehicle.
- Original ambient/electronic score is generated by `npm run assets:audio` to `packages/video-engine/public/audio/sports-car-score.wav`.

The car is an original stylized procedural model, not a licensed or manufacturer-accurate vehicle and does not depict a real brand.
