# Creative brief — A Bird's Journey

## Objective
Create a short, purely visual 2D animation that makes the freedom and physical grace of a small bird in flight feel immediate and uplifting.

## Audience and response
General audience. The viewer should feel calm, wonder and a sense of lift as the bird moves from a quiet perch into open sky.

## Format
- 24 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps, MP4/H.264.
- Wordless animation; no narration or explanatory text.
- Original vector illustration and procedural motion. No paid services or third-party footage.

## Creative thesis
A single bird turns a quiet dawn into a journey: it launches from a branch, rides the air above a valley, sweeps close past the viewer, then becomes a small silhouette against the evening horizon.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The bird is clearly readable as a bird, with wings visibly flapping and gliding.
2. The four shots show distinct compositions and settings, not one repeated card or background.
3. The take-off, valley flight, close pass/bank and horizon glide are all visibly animated.
4. Motion is smooth, intentional and coherent across the sequence.
5. The final output is a playable 24-second 1080p/30fps MP4 with no render/codec errors.
