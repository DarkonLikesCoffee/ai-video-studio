# Creative direction — A Bird's Journey

**Selected territory:** graphic-led 2D naturalist short film. A hand-drawn silhouette bird is the single visual anchor; layered landscapes and warm/cool atmospheric gradients evolve as it travels. A photographic montage was rejected because no licensed footage is necessary for this stylized short; 3D was rejected because it adds complexity without improving the readable flight action.

**Visual system:** painterly vector shapes, restrained detail and soft atmospheric gradients. Cool blue-green dawn and valley tones shift toward warm amber at the horizon. The bird remains a dark, crisp silhouette with a small ochre beak and articulated feather shapes.

**Motion language:** articulated wing strokes alternate between flap and glide, with a small body bob and banking rotation. Clouds drift at a different rate from mountain layers to imply parallax. Cuts are motivated by changes in scale and vantage point.

**Sound:** deliberately silent visual short. No unlicensed music, sound effects or narration.

**Composition:** custom Remotion `BirdFlight` (2D). It is selected over `ProjectVideo` because the latter applies a recurring editorial scene layout; this film needs per-shot vector environments and a custom animated bird rig. No WebGL is used.

**Delivery:** 24 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 MP4.

**Limits:** original stylized bird, not a photorealistic species depiction.
